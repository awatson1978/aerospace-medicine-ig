### Space Telemedicine (WP16)

FHIR profiles for telemedicine in spaceflight operations, based on ISRO-SCTIMST MoU (2025-2026) and ISS telemedicine operations.

#### Profiles
- **TelemedicineEncounter** — Telemedicine encounter with modality and communication latency
- **TelemedicineConsultRequest** — Consultation request from CMO to ground specialist
- **TelemedicineCommunication** — Clinical data and image transmission requests
- **TelemedicineDevice** — Medical equipment for telemedicine (remote ultrasound, digital stethoscope)
