### Crew Rescue and Emergency Evacuation (WP15)

FHIR profiles for crew rescue and emergency evacuation scenarios, based on NASA OIG audit "Ruled Out Crew Rescue" (2026) and Artemis lunar landing analysis.

#### Profiles
- **SpaceEmergencyCondition** — Emergency diagnosis (depressurization, fire, toxic atmosphere)
- **EmergencyEvacuationProcedure** — Evacuation or abort action (Dragon return, Soyuz, safe haven)
- **EmergencyRiskAssessment** — Probability prediction for emergency event types
- **EmergencyCarePlan** — Contingency plan addressing identified emergency conditions
