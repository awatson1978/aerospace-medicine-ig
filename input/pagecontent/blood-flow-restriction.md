### Blood Flow Restriction Training (WP12)

FHIR profiles for blood flow restriction training (BFRT) as a microgravity exercise countermeasure, based on evidence from npj Microgravity (2026).

#### Profiles
- **BFRTDevice** — Occlusion cuff device (pneumatic, intelligent, elastic wrap)
- **BFRTProcedure** — BFRT session with occlusion pressure and gravity context
- **BFRTActivityDefinition** — Prescribed BFRT protocol with sex-specific adaptations
- **BFRTOutcomeObservation** — Muscle CSA, bone biomarkers (P1NP, CTX), muscle thickness
- **BFRTCarePlan** — Countermeasure plan addressing bone loss and muscle atrophy
