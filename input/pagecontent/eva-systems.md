### EVA Systems Expansion (WP18)

FHIR profiles for xEVAS suit provisioning and redundancy assessment, based on NASA OIG IG-26-006 (xEVAS audit) and Harwood MIT thesis (2026).

#### Profiles
- **XEVASSuitDefinition** — xEVAS suit definition with model, capabilities, and operational parameters
- **EVASuitProvisioningRecord** — Suit provisioning logistics and delivery records
- **SuitRedundancyAssessment** — EVA suit redundancy status, consumables, and maintenance tracking
