### Space Omics Expansion (WP17)

FHIR profiles for multi-omics astronaut molecular profiling and biobanking, based on SOMA / international astronaut biobank (Nature, 2024-2026).

#### Profiles
- **MultiOmicsObservation** — Multi-omics analysis with platform, read count, and quality metrics
- **SpaceMolecularSequence** — Molecular sequence data from astronaut genomic analysis
- **OmicsRiskAssessment** — Personalized risk from molecular profiles
- **PersonalizedCountermeasurePlan** — Individualized countermeasures informed by omics data
