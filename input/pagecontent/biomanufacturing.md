### Biomanufacturing (WP14)

FHIR profiles for in-situ biomanufacturing and bioregenerative life support systems, based on Bell et al. (Space Policy, May 2026) and extraterrestrial infrastructure reviews.

#### Profiles
- **BioreactorDevice** — Bioreactor device with volume, temperature, and culture parameters
- **BiomanufacturingProcedure** — ISRU bioprocessing, algae cultivation, fermentation
- **BLiSSResourceOutput** — O2 production, food output, water recycling, CO2 fixation rates
- **BiologicalFeedstock** — Biological feedstock or bioproduct substance
