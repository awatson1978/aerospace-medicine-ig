### Planetary Protection (WP13)

FHIR profiles for planetary protection microbial contamination control, based on Fujimura et al. (Applied and Environmental Microbiology, April 2026).

#### Profiles
- **MicrobialContaminationCondition** — Contamination diagnosis with COSPAR protection category
- **MicrobialContaminationRisk** — Forward contamination risk prediction
- **SterilizationProcedure** — UV, DHMR, VHP sterilization with process parameters
- **MicrobialViabilityAssay** — MPN and CFU counts with contaminant type identification
